Day 2 · 9:30–10:30 AM

Photo Hunt Team Challenge

Teams race through Preah Khan Temple to capture creative shots from a shared checklist. Fun, fast, and full of team spirit.

Rules

  1. Teams of 5–6, assigned at check-in.
  2. Each team receives a photo checklist at Preah Khan Temple.
  3. 60 minutes to capture every shot on the list.
  4. Submit photos via the team WhatsApp group before the deadline.
  5. Judged on creativity, teamwork, and completeness.
